## Data and intellectual honesty

- Commercial state at plan creation: **0 customers, $0 actual revenue, $0 opening cash, $0 committed financing**.
- Market demand, monthly customer additions, subscription tiers, unit costs, churn, financial returns and all forecasts are **hypotheses** within the sponsor-generated model, **not measured business facts**.
- Plan assumes unpaid founder work ($0 founder salary) and excludes tax, legal and insurance, so modeled profit is not robust take-home income.
- The synthetic *technical* example (4 / 1 / 3 payment rows) is separate from financial sales assumptions and does not measure actual avoided financial losses.
- Actual opening cash of $0 implies **no funded runway now**, even when an unfinanced forecast later shows positive cumulative cash; early funding gaps require cash that is not already available.
